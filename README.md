# ERA RIVALS — Arena Edition

An original four-sport, browser-playable sports career prototype, created for Xavier Matos.

## Play

- **index.html:** New visual MYCAREER hub and game launcher
- **arena.js / arena.css:** Real-time, short-form basketball, American football, soccer and baseball gameplay, athlete customization, training, franchise branding, and career progress
- **basketball-3d.html:** Separate procedural WebGL basketball court with player movement and hold/release shooting
- **classic.html:** Preserved four-sport fantasy drafts and simulation-based Manager / Player Career modes

**Controls:** WASD or arrows move; hold/release Space to shoot, swing or throw; J special/pass; F tackle/steal; P pause. Touch controls and partial gamepad support.

Career saves live in your browser's localStorage for this site. College and professional paths only, no high-school starts. Offline copies may not share saves with the website.

## Scope and attribution

This is an **unofficial, original, independent sports-game prototype**, not an official 2K, Madden, EA FC or MLB The Show product. The primary four-sport arena uses original procedural broadcast-style 2D/2.5D graphics; the basketball training court uses WebGL. It is **not** a full 3D AAA simulation of all four sports. School and professional team names are a limited reference catalog; fictional players and attributes are for gameplay purposes and **not** official rosters or ratings. No licensed player likenesses, official crests, or proprietary game code are included.
