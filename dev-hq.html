Position-specific rating audit

Compare players only within useful position groups. A QB, center-back, point guard, and striker should not share one generic rating recipe.

Rating design rules

Position-specific attributes
Define weighted attributes by position, not one overall formula.
Era-aware context
Account for era, role, peak seasons, longevity, and league environment.
Elite legends
All-time greats should have elite ratings in their defining strengths, without making every attribute a 99.
Outlier scan
Flag unexpected low ratings, missing values, duplicate players, and position mismatches.

Position-based Hall of Fame comparison

WEEKLY DEVELOPMENT MILESTONE

Milestone 01 · Position-specific ratings audit

READY TO START · NOT COMPLETED

Why it matters: ratings drive gameplay balance, roster value, draft quality, trade logic, and the credibility of all-time teams. Fixing the rating foundation prevents bad data from spreading into every mode.

Step-by-step checklist

Specific test plan

1 · Position separation
Compare top players at QB, WR, RB, PG, C, striker, defender, pitcher, and other positions. Check that each rating emphasizes the right skills.
2 · Legend sanity check
Check Brady, Jerry Rice, Michael Jordan, Lionel Messi, and position-appropriate all-time greats. Confirm elite defining traits and no unexplained weak overall rating.
3 · Current player sanity check
Sample starters, backups, rookies, and role players. Avoid rating everyone like a superstar.
4 · Data integrity
Search for nulls, NaN, ratings outside the allowed range, duplicate IDs, and wrong-position attributes.
5 · Gameplay regression
Simulate games and verify standings, player value, depth charts, and draft order still behave sensibly.
