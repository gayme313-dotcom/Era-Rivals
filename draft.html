ERA RIVALS / RULES

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Choose one of the four sports.
  2. Choose your position. Only players from that position appear.
  3. For basketball, choose a decade to view era-specific players and ratings.
  4. Spin each attribute or use SPIN ALL ATTRIBUTES.
  5. The player you land on supplies that attribute and its F–S tier.
  6. Your overall tier is calculated from the players you spun.

No manual ratings or build creator. Your player is made from the players you get.
